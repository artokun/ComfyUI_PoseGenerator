## What is published

The public integration and registry archive contain no gallery image collection.
Existing research assets, derived English labels, bulk-download tooling, and
screenshots depicting the gallery stay local while permission is unresolved.
The original registry/social artwork was generated separately for this integration;
it does not contain copied gallery images.

Use your own licensed local images and catalog. No gallery downloads occur during
node import or workflow execution. Any separately licensed material in the source
collection retains its own attribution and terms; this node currently selects only
pose and standard outfit entries, not portraits or historical/anime collections.
